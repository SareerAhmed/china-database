# Contributing

Resume by reading the five memory files listed in README. Work region by region. Search canonical and Chinese names plus aliases in all three database formats before adding a university. Keep one canonical ID per institution; do not merge similarly named universities.

Use direct official university/CSC evidence. Record its actual intake year, retrieval limitations and source ID. Distinguish government CSC B from provincial/university scholarships and university-local A/B/C labels. Do not infer IELTS waivers, fees, agency codes or benefits. Enter Unknown when uncertain. Restrict historical claims to the evidence year.

Edit UNIVERSITIES.md first; synchronize using scripts/sync.py and validate with --check. Update sources, log and region/progress counts each batch. A regional completion needs a documented candidate sweep and exclusions; finding major universities is insufficient. Record an exact resume task.

At 20 pending unique records validate exports and staged content, commit only relevant files and push. Update published counters only after confirmed push success; preserve all work and counters on failure. Never add credentials, applicant records or personal contact details. Recheck announcements periodically after discovery coverage finishes.
